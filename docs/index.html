Important Notice

The circle sizes and colours on this map are chosen purely for visual clarity. They do not indicate any actual radiation risk. Ambient gamma dose rates across the UK are consistently well within safe limits. Variation between stations reflects natural background differences — geology, altitude, and weather — not hazard levels.

This is an independent visualisation of publicly available government data. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced in collaboration with the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, RREMS, or any other government agency.
